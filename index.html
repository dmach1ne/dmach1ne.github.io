Research

I believe that achieving AGI requires learning paradigms that enable agents to adapt and improve through autonomous experience, much like Reinforcement Learning (RL). In particular, I am interested in Continual RL and Meta RL, which aim to enable agents to learn reusable knowledge from their histories and apply it to new environments, potentially improving the sample efficiency of RL algorithms. Recently, I have become interested in learning intrinsic rewards online to enable agents to explore rationally and adapt to changing environments.

Revisiting Temporal Regularization for Smooth Control in Deep Reinforcement Learning
SungJae Ahn, Jeong Woon Lee, Kyoleen Kwak, Hyoseok Hwang
Submitted to ICRA 2027
project page / paper / code

In this work, we show that the temporal penalty, previously considered unrelated to spatial smoothness, can in fact induce this very property through shared next states, improving robustness to observation noise. Our extensive experiments show that, by avoiding direct spatial regularization, CATS can achieve both forms of smoothness while better preserving task performance.

Analyzing Reasoning Diversity in Self-Distillation
Advanced Deep Learning, Spring 2026
presentation

SDPO uses a single successful rollout to supervise others, explicitly reinforcing a narrow set of reasoning strategies. We demonstrated that utilizing only text feedback from failed rollouts can encourage diverse strategies. We also found that, together with SDPO's top-K approximation, the direction of KL divergence has different effects depending on the outcome. For example, when the student fails, the reverse KL used by SDPO provides a weaker learning signal than forward KL for selecting new words because the student already assigns low probability to promising tokens.
